Science and Technology

Focuses on cutting-edge scientific research and technological innovation. Programs include Computer Science, Information Technology, Environmental Science, and Engineering.

Departments:

  • Computer Science: BSc Software Engineering, BSc Computer Science, BSc Computer Technology
  • Information Technology: Bachelor in IT, Bachelor in Business IT

Business and Economics

Offering programs in Business Administration, Economics, Marketing, and Finance, this faculty prepares students for leadership roles in the corporate world.

Education

Dedicated to training future educators, this faculty provides programs in Primary and Secondary Education, Curriculum Development, and Educational Leadership.

Arts and Humanities

This faculty nurtures creativity and critical thinking through programs in Literature, History, Philosophy, and Cultural Studies.

Health Sciences

Focused on healthcare education, this faculty offers programs in Nursing, Public Health, and Medical Laboratory Sciences.
