About Mwaki University

Igniting knowledge, transforming lives since 2010

History & Background

Mwaki University was founded with a vision to ignite knowledge and innovation across Kenya and beyond. Established in the early 2010s through collaboration between local leaders, educators, and national stakeholders, the institution was created to expand access to higher education while fostering community development.

The name Mwaki — meaning "fire" in Kiswahili — symbolizes enlightenment, resilience, and the spark of discovery. From its humble beginnings with a few academic programs, Mwaki University has grown into a multidisciplinary institution offering degrees in Science, Technology, Business, Education, and the Arts.

Located on a serene campus designed to inspire learning and research, Mwaki University emphasizes both academic excellence and cultural heritage. Its mission is to nurture graduates who are not only skilled professionals but also responsible citizens committed to innovation and service.

Today, Mwaki University stands as a beacon of opportunity, empowering students to transform challenges into solutions and to carry forward the flame of knowledge into the future.
